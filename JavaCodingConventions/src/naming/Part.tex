\part{Naming Conventions}\label{sec:NamingConventions}

\begin{quote}
\bdq{As names have power, words have power.} \\
Patrick Rothfuss: \textit{The Name of the Wind} \autocite{Rothfuss:NameOfTheWind}
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
\bdq{There is no other power. No other name.} \\
Ursula K. LeGuin: \textit{A Wizard of Earthsea} \autocite{LeGuin:WizardOfEarthsea}
\end{quote}

Magic is a topic for several fantasy stories, and in some of these stories, knowing the real names of persons and things means to have control over them. Names have power!

The same kind of magic lies in the names we gave to the elements of our programs.
